# Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard

A complete end-to-end Business Intelligence project using the **Global Superstore dataset**, transforming raw retail data into executive-ready insights through **Power BI**, **DAX**, and structured **data storytelling**.

## Objective

Enable sales leaders, product managers, and regional stakeholders to:
- Identify key performance drivers  
- Isolate loss areas  
- Uncover growth opportunities  
...using a well-designed analytics solution.


## Project Highlights

- Cleaned and modelled over **51,000 retail transactions** across product categories, customer segments, and geographies  
- Developed more than **10 custom DAX measures** including Profit Margin %, YoY Growth, Return Impact, and Discount Effectiveness  
- Built a fully interactive **Power BI dashboard** with slicers, bookmarks, drill-through pages, and thematic consistency  
- Delivered a **structured case study** with measurable business insights and recommendations  
- Emphasised **usability and storytelling** for both technical and non-technical audiences

## Case Study Summary

**[Download Full Case Study (PDF)](./reports/Global_Superstore_Case_Study.pdf) [Ongoing...]**

### Problem Statement
Leadership teams lacked visibility into performance trends due to fragmented reports and flat spreadsheets.

### Solution
A single, dynamic Power BI dashboard offering:
- Executive-level KPIs  
- Drill-down into regional and product-specific performance  

### Key Insights

- **Technology** category generates 45% of profits but has high return rates in the **Western region**
- **Office Supplies** perform poorly in the **South** with a negative margin
- Adjusting **discount strategies** in key categories could increase profitability by **12% annually**
